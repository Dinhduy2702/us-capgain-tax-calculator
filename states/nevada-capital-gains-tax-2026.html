How Nevada Taxes Capital Gains in 2026

Nevada charges 0% state capital gains tax in 2026 on stocks, options, cryptocurrency, startup equity, and real estate sales. Protected by Nevada's state constitution prohibiting personal income taxes, residents of Las Vegas, Henderson, Summerlin, and Reno pay ONLY federal IRS capital gains taxes (0%/15%/20% LTCG) plus the 3.8% NIIT surtax above $200k/$250k MAGI.

Combining Federal LTCG, 3.8% NIIT & Nevada State Tax

Living right across the Sierra Nevada from California makes Nevada the single most popular West Coast tax-migration destination for crypto investors, startup founders, and retirees: on a $500,000 long-term equity or crypto liquidation, establishing bona fide Nevada residency BEFORE the sale date saves roughly $55,000 to $66,500+ in California state capital gains tax! Just remember that California audits departing residents aggressively—your move to Nevada must be permanent and well-documented prior to executing the sale.

4 Legal Ways Nevada Investors Cut Capital Gains Tax in 2026

  • If moving from California to Las Vegas or Reno before a major stock/crypto sale, complete all domicile steps (NV driver's license, home purchase/lease, voter registration, >183 days outside CA) BEFORE selling.
  • Harvest gains inside the 2026 federal 0% LTCG bracket ($48,350 Single / $96,700 Married taxable income) for $0 federal + $0 Nevada tax.
  • Remember real estate physically located inside California is still California-sourced even if you live in Nevada—whereas publicly traded stocks and crypto follow your state of residence on the trade date!
  • Pair Nevada's 0% capital gains tax with a 1031 Exchange on investment properties to defer federal LTCG and 25% depreciation recapture.

Frequently Asked Questions: Nevada Capital Gains Tax

Do I pay any Nevada state tax when selling crypto or stocks in 2026?

No. Nevada has 0% personal income tax and 0% state capital gains tax.

If I move from California to Nevada and sell my stocks, do I still owe California tax?

Publicly traded stocks and crypto are generally taxed by your state of legal residence on the date of sale—provided you truly severed California domicile BEFORE selling (unlike CA real estate, which remains CA-sourced).

Does Nevada charge a state transfer tax when selling a house?

Nevada charges zero capital gains tax, though sellers pay a small Real Property Transfer Tax (~$2.55 per $500 in Clark County / $2.05 in Washoe County) at closing.
