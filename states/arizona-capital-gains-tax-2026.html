How Arizona Taxes Capital Gains in 2026

That slashes Arizona's effective state tax rate on long-term stocks, crypto, and real estate gains down to an astonishing 1.875% (75% × 2.50%)—with zero local city tax!

Frequently Asked Questions: Arizona Capital Gains Tax

How does Arizona's 25% Long-Term Capital Gains subtraction work in 2026?

Arizona lets you subtract 25% of net long-term capital gains derived from assets acquired after December 31, 2011—reducing the 2.50% flat tax to an effective 1.875% rate.

Does Arizona's 25% LTCG subtraction apply to crypto and real estate?

Yes, as long as the asset was acquired after December 31, 2011 and qualifies as a federal long-term capital gain (> 1 year holding period).

Do Phoenix or Scottsdale charge local capital gains tax?

No. Arizona has zero local municipal or county capital gains taxes.
