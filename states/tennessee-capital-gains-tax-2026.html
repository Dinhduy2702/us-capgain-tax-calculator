How Tennessee Taxes Capital Gains in 2026

Tennessee imposes 0% state capital gains tax in 2026 on stocks, ETFs, crypto, business exits, and real estate sales. Having permanently repealed the old Hall Income Tax, Tennessee residents in Nashville, Franklin, Knoxville, and Chattanooga owe ONLY federal IRS capital gains tax (0%, 15%, or 20% long-term) plus the 3.8% NIIT surtax above $200k/$250k MAGI—with zero local city capital gains taxes.

Frequently Asked Questions: Tennessee Capital Gains Tax

Does Tennessee tax capital gains on stocks, crypto, or real estate in 2026?

No. Tennessee has 0% state income tax and 0% state capital gains tax—you owe only federal IRS taxes.

What is the maximum combined capital gains tax rate in Tennessee?

23.8% for long-term capital gains (20% federal top bracket + 3.8% NIIT) and 40.8% for short-term gains.

Does Tennessee still have the Hall Income Tax on dividends or investments?

No. Tennessee permanently repealed the Hall Income Tax—all dividends, interest, and capital gains are 0% state-taxed.
