How Texas Taxes Capital Gains in 2026

Texas charges 0% state capital gains tax in 2026 on stocks, ETFs, cryptocurrency, business exits, and real estate sales. Protected by the Texas Constitution—which explicitly prohibits a personal state income tax without a statewide voter referendum—Texas investors only owe federal IRS capital gains taxes (0%, 15%, or 20% for long-term assets held over 1 year, or ordinary federal brackets up to 37% for short-term trades) plus the 3.8% federal Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT) once Modified Adjusted Gross Income exceeds $200,000 Single or $250,000 Married.

Combining Federal LTCG, 3.8% NIIT & Texas State Tax

Compared to California (13.3%) or New York (10.9% plus NYC local tax), living in Texas saves an investor $9,300 to $13,300 on every $100,000 of realized capital gain. On a $500,000 startup equity liquidation, crypto cycle exit, or rental property sale, establishing bona fide Texas residency before the sale date can easily preserve $50,000 to $65,000+ in net after-tax wealth. Because Texas imposes zero state tax, your entire tax-optimization focus shifts to mastering federal IRS brackets: keeping holding periods past 366 days, harvesting gains inside the 2026 federal 0% LTCG bracket (up to $48,350 Single / $96,700 Married taxable income), and managing MAGI around the NIIT cliff.

4 Legal Ways Texas Investors Cut Capital Gains Tax in 2026

  • Use the 0% Federal LTCG Bracket in gap years or early retirement—because Texas adds 0%, you can realize up to $96,700 (Married) in taxable long-term gains completely tax-free.
  • Wait for Day 366 whenever possible: in a 0%-tax state, the spread between federal Short-Term (up to 37%) and Long-Term (15%–20%) is your single biggest tax lever.
  • If relocating from CA or NY to Texas before a major stock or business sale, sever old-state domicile completely (driver's license, homestead exemption, >183 days in TX) before executing the trade.
  • For Texas investment properties, remember that while state tax is 0%, federal 25% Depreciation Recapture and federal LTCG still apply—making a 1031 Exchange valuable for large portfolios.

Frequently Asked Questions: Texas Capital Gains Tax

Do I pay any Texas state tax when selling stocks, crypto, or a house in 2026?

No. Texas has no personal income tax and no state capital gains tax. You only report and pay federal capital gains tax to the IRS.

What is the maximum combined capital gains tax rate for a Texas resident?

The maximum federal long-term rate in Texas is 23.8% (20% top federal LTCG + 3.8% NIIT). Short-term gains top out at 40.8% (37% ordinary + 3.8% NIIT).

Do Texas property taxes offset the 0% capital gains advantage?

Texas has higher annual property taxes than the national average, so long-term real estate holders should factor annual property tax into cash flow, though capital appreciation at sale remains 0% state-taxed.
