How North Carolina Taxes Capital Gains in 2026

North Carolina taxes all realized capital gains at its low, flat 4.25% state income tax rate in 2026 (scheduled to step down toward 3.99% under enacted state tax reforms) with zero local Charlotte or Raleigh municipal taxes. Because North Carolina starts with Federal AGI, every federal tax-reduction tool—Tax-Loss Harvesting, $3,000 W-2 loss offsets, indefinite carryforwards, Section 121 home exclusions ($250k/$500k), and 1031 Exchanges—flows straight through to your NC state return.

Combining Federal LTCG, 3.8% NIIT & North Carolina State Tax

For a Single filer earning $150,000 in Charlotte or the Research Triangle realizing a $100,000 long-term stock gain, North Carolina collects just $4,250—saving over $5,000 compared to California and $6,000+ compared to New York City. Note that North Carolina does not offer a special LTCG rate discount, so holding past Day 366 remains essential to capture federal 0%/15%/20% brackets.

Frequently Asked Questions: North Carolina Capital Gains Tax

Yes. Because NC starts from Federal AGI, federal capital loss offsets and carryforwards apply automatically.
