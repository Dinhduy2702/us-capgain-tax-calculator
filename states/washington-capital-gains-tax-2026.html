How Washington Taxes Capital Gains in 2026

Washington State has one of the most unique capital gains tax laws in America in 2026. While Washington levies 0% tax on W-2 wages, 1099 freelance income, and short-term capital gains, the state enforces a dedicated excise tax of 7% (rising to 9.9% on gains exceeding $1 million under recent state tier expansions) specifically on net Long-Term Capital Gains from stocks, bonds, and crypto above the inflation-adjusted annual exemption (approximately $270,000 per individual or married couple).

Combining Federal LTCG, 3.8% NIIT & Washington State Tax

For most Seattle and Bellevue tech workers selling under $270,000 in net long-term gains in a calendar year, Washington's state capital gains tax is $0—a massive advantage over California's 13.3%. Crucially, Washington's capital gains excise tax completely exempts ALL real estate sales (primary homes and investment properties alike), retirement accounts (401(k), IRA, Roth IRA), and qualifying small business sales. However, for Amazon, Microsoft, or startup employees liquidating $500,000 to $2,000,000+ of appreciated long-term stock in a single year, crossing the $270,000 cliff triggers an immediate 7% to 9.9% state bill on the excess.

4 Legal Ways Washington Investors Cut Capital Gains Tax in 2026

  • Harvest up to $270,000 of Long-Term Capital Gains EVERY calendar year in Washington to reset your cost basis at 0% state tax (note: Married couples share a single $270k exemption, not double!).
  • Split a $500,000 stock liquidation across late December and early January to capture two separate $270,000 annual Washington exemptions—cutting state tax from ~$16,100 to $0.
  • Remember that vested RSUs sold on vest day generate W-2 ordinary income (0% WA tax) and $0 capital gain—never hold vested RSUs blindly into a >$270k gain cliff.
  • Leverage Washington's 100% real estate exemption: selling a Seattle rental property incurs 0% WA capital gains tax (though WA REET transfer tax applies at closing).

Frequently Asked Questions: Washington Capital Gains Tax

Do Married Filing Jointly couples get double the $270,000 exemption in Washington?

No. Unlike federal brackets, Washington grants only ONE annual standard deduction (~$270,000 inflation-adjusted) per return whether you file Single or Married Filing Jointly.

Does Washington's capital gains tax apply to selling a house or rental property?

No. All real estate sales are 100% exempt from Washington's capital gains excise tax (though sellers pay Washington's graduated Real Estate Excise Tax / REET at closing).

Are short-term capital gains or RSU vests taxed by Washington State?

No. Washington's excise tax applies strictly to federal Long-Term Capital Gains. W-2 wages, RSU vests, and short-term capital gains face 0% Washington state income tax.
